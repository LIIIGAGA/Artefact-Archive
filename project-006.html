// Overview// 简介

Osaider is a narrative-driven architectural interaction game. Set within an ocean palace sealed from the outside world, its people have no past, no history — they are simply the forgotten remnants of a world that has moved on. Life follows a single cycle: by day they manufacture weapons, by night those weapons are melted down and reborn as raw materials, ready to be produced again the following day. The cycle exists not for war, but as a performative ritual of deterrence.

The nation is built around the fear of a mysterious enemy: Osaider. But as players traverse the palace and piece together its fragmented history, a deeper question emerges — what if Osaider does not exist at all?

Two factions shape the world in secret: the Archivists control official memory, determining what is worthy of being remembered; the Whalemen are an underground society who have seen through the myth, quietly planning their escape. Players navigate between these forces through a Dice Check system — every collectible item carries a hidden Ideology value (IDEO) that shapes narrative outcomes and unlocks faction-specific storylets.

How to break the cycle, find a path to the future, and retell this buried tale anew, how to give meaning to the loop itself — all of that is yours to decide.

Osaider 是一款叙事驱动的建筑互动游戏。这是一座与外界隔绝的海上宫殿，这里的人们没有过去，没有历史，他们只是一群被世界遗忘的神之弃民。国民的生命遵循同一个循环：白天制造武器，夜晚武器被熔毁、还原为原材料，次日重新投入生产。这个循环存在的意义，不是为了战争，而是一场维持威慑形象的荒诞仪式。

这个国度建立在对一个神秘敌人的恐惧之上：Osaider。但随着玩家在宫殿中穿行、拼凑起碎片化的历史，一个更深的疑问逐渐浮现——如果 Osaider 根本不存在呢？

两股势力在暗中角力：档案官掌控官方记忆，决定什么值得被铭记；鲸人是看穿神话的地下组织，正悄然筹备一场出逃。玩家通过骰子检定系统在两者之间周旋——每件可收集物品都藏有隐性意识形态值（IDEO），影响叙事走向与派系支线的解锁。

如何打破循环，通往未来，又重新讲述这段尘封往事，将意义赋予循环本身，都由你来决定。
